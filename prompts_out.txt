--- CREATIVE 1 ---
BRIEF: Using the AIDA framework to drive top of funnel discovery, this static 1:1 ad focuses on the Desire and Identity archetype by showcasing the aspirational, radiant after-state of beauty. The visual hero is a bright, airy, high-end lifestyle photograph of a person with effortlessly luminous, glowing skin, basking in warm morning sunlight and holding a clear glass of the vibrant, translucent coral-pink collagen beverage next to the elegant matte-white Wellbeing Nutrition tub. The clean layout uses a soft Wellness Cream (#fff9eb) background to evoke a premium, natural morning ritual feel. The short, high-contrast on-image headline is set in elegant Tenor Sans typography and reads: 'Drink your morning glow.' A soft call to action button is styled as a sleek, black, sharp-cornered rectangle in the bottom corner reading 'Explore the collection.' Keep this creative on-brand by using the brand's signature soft pink (#FBE7F2) accents, a light grid overlay with an opacity of 0.1 for structural sophistication, and avoiding heavy text to let the aspirational, glowing skin imagery dominate.

Design requirements (apply to this ad):
- Follow the instructions by the director.
- Make the ad design, layout, typography on-branded, but should look premium, editorial-like.
- Establish a clear visual hierarchy: one dominant focal point the eye lands on
  first, then the supporting message, then the CTA.
- Keep strong figure/ground contrast. If text sits over imagery, place a scrim or
  overlay behind it so it stays fully legible.
- Place the focal subject on a rule-of-thirds line; leave generous whitespace and
  avoid clutter.
- Use a limited palette anchored to the brand colors (~60-30-10), reserving one
  accent color for the call to action.
- Keep 20% space all around the creative for the ad to look cleaner
- Group and align elements cleanly (Gestalt); use at most two type styles.

--- CREATIVE 2 ---
BRIEF: Using the PAS framework to target top of funnel discovery, this static 1:1 ad addresses the Problem Solver archetype by dramatizing the daily friction of tired, dehydrated skin. The visual hero is a bold, split-screen graphic: the left side is a muted, desaturated Mist Gray (#f2f4f7) representing dull, lifeless skin texture, while the right side is a high-energy, vibrant coral-pink splash of the dissolved collagen drink next to the clean white 'glow' tub, representing instant revival. The high-impact on-image headline is written in clean, medium-weight Circular Std and reads: 'Tired of dull morning skin?' A pill-shaped accent tag at the top in Primary Brand Orange (#ff5000) highlights 'Korean Marine Collagen.' The soft call to action at the bottom reads 'See how it works.' Keep this creative on-brand by pairing the clinical Mist Gray with the vibrant action orange gradient, utilizing sharp miter-stroke icons to signal scientific validation, and ensuring the product packaging is shown clearly at the center of the color transition.

--- CREATIVE 3 ---
BRIEF: Using the BAB (Before-After-Bridge) framework for top of funnel discovery, this static 1:1 ad appeals to the Upgrade and Optimization archetype by visualizing the superior quality of premium ingredients. The visual visual hero is a highly styled, minimalist flat-lay on a warm Sand Beige (#F4EBE1) surface, featuring the matte-white 'glow' tub surrounded by clean, artistic placements of its premium key ingredients: a branch of fresh goji berries, a sleek glass containing the vibrant, translucent coral-pink dissolved beverage, and subtle, elegant waves of clean water. The layout uses a sophisticated editorial look to expose the gap between standard supplements and this premium formulation. The short, geometric headline is set in clean Circular Std and reads: 'Upgrade your daily beauty standard.' The soft call to action sits on a sharp-edged rectangular button in Premium Black (#000000) reading 'Learn more.' Keep the ad on-brand by using the warm, organic Sand Beige background, keeping text elements under 20% of the canvas area, and emphasizing the premium, dermatologist-recommended aesthetic through airy spacing and precise, minimalist layout design.
