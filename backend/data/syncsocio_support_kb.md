## 1. PLATFORM OVERVIEW

**Q: What is SyncSocio?**
A: SyncSocio is an all-in-one AI content workspace built for Instagram growth. It plans your content mix, generates on-brand images, carousels, reels, and captions, schedules posts, runs DM automations, manages WhatsApp/email campaigns, and surfaces real-time analytics — all from one dashboard.

**Q: Do I need a website to use SyncSocio?**
A: No. SyncSocio is Instagram-first. You only need an Instagram Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page. Everything else is handled inside the platform.

**Q: What do I need to get started?**
A: You need: (1) An Instagram Business or Creator account, (2) that account linked to a Facebook Page, (3) a SyncSocio account (sign up free), and (4) connect Instagram from Settings > Connected Accounts.

## 2. IMAGE GENERATION

**Q: How do I generate an image?**
A: Go to the Studio section in your dashboard. Choose your content type (Image, Carousel, Video, etc.), fill in optional details (topic, product), and click Generate. Credits are deducted per generation.

**Q: What image types can I generate?**
A: Static Ad Images, Multi-slide Carousels, UGC-style Videos, Product Showcase Videos, E-commerce Listing Images, Festival Posts, WhatsApp campaign graphics, Photoshoot-style product photos, and Email campaign copy.

**Q: What aspect ratios are available?**
A: 1:1 (Square), 4:5 (Portrait), 9:16 (Story/Reel), 16:9 (Landscape), and 2:3 (Classic).

**Q: Why is my image not generating / stuck loading?**
A: This usually happens if: (a) your internet is slow, (b) you have insufficient credits, or (c) the AI server is temporarily busy. Wait 30 seconds and try again. If it keeps failing, please raise a support ticket.

**Q: Can I upload my own product photos?**
A: Yes. In the generation form there is an "Upload Image" option. Upload a product photo and the AI will incorporate it into the creative.

**Q: How does the AI know my brand style?**
A: During onboarding (or in Settings > Brand Profile), you set your brand name, category, colors, tone, USP, and visual vibe. The AI uses this "Brand DNA" for every generation automatically.

**Q: Can I generate content in regional languages?**
A: Yes. Supported languages: English, Hinglish, Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, and Nepali. Select your language in the generation form.

**Q: What is Photoshoot Mode?**
A: Photoshoot mode generates professional-quality product photographs. Upload your product image and select a background or scene. The AI renders it as a studio-quality photoshoot — no physical setup needed. Available on all plans.

**Q: What is Clone Mode?**
A: Clone Mode lets you replicate the visual style of any reference image. Upload a reference and SyncSocio generates new content that matches its aesthetic — great for feed consistency. Available on Growth and Pro plans.

**Q: What is Sprint (Bulk Generation)?**
A: Sprint lets you generate multiple posts at once from a single brief — great for creating a week's or month's worth of content in minutes. Available on Growth and Pro plans.

**Q: What is the All-In-One Drop mode?**
A: All-In-One generates a complete marketing pack from a single product description: static image, carousel, video/reel, and caption — all at once. Available on Growth and Pro plans.

**Q: What is an E-Comm Listing?**
A: This mode generates marketplace-ready product images, optimized for product listing pages on e-commerce platforms. Available on all plans.

**Q: What are Festival Posts?**
A: Festival Posts are calendar-aware content. SyncSocio shows upcoming Indian and global festivals. Click any festival to instantly generate a themed, branded post — image, caption, and hashtags — in one click.

**Q: My generated image has wrong colors / doesn't match my brand.**
A: Go to Settings > Brand Profile and check your Primary Color and Secondary Color are set correctly. After updating, re-generate — the AI will pick up the new colors.

## 3. CONTENT STRATEGY AND PLANNING

**Q: What is the Content Plan?**
A: The Content Plan gives you an AI-recommended weekly posting schedule based on your niche, growth goals, and audience activity. It tells you what type of content to post each day and at what time.

**Q: What is the Content Mix?**
A: The Content Mix shows the recommended ratio of content types for your niche (e.g., 40% educational, 30% promotional, 30% engagement). It helps you maintain an algorithm-friendly variety.

**Q: What is Autopilot?**
A: Autopilot lets SyncSocio automatically generate and queue content for you on a recurring schedule. Once set up, it runs in the background and keeps your feed active without manual effort.

**Q: How does SyncSocio decide the best time for me to post?**
A: SyncSocio connects to the Instagram Graph API and analyzes when your specific followers are online. The "Peak Hour" shown in your dashboard is personalized to your audience. This feature is available on Growth and Pro plans.

## 4. SCHEDULING AND FEED

**Q: How do I schedule a post?**
A: After generating a post, click "Save and Schedule." Pick your date and time, confirm, and the post will be queued for automatic publishing to your Instagram account.

**Q: How many posts can I schedule?**
A: Starter plan: up to 30 posts. Growth and Pro plans: unlimited post scheduling.

**Q: My scheduled post didn't publish. Why?**
A: Most common causes: (1) Instagram token expired — go to Settings > Connected Accounts and reconnect your Instagram account, (2) your Meta app review is pending. Check the Connected Accounts tab for a status indicator. If everything looks fine, please raise a ticket.

**Q: Where can I see all my posts?**
A: Go to Feed in the left sidebar. You can see your live Instagram posts, scheduled posts, and saved drafts.

## 5. ANALYTICS

**Q: What analytics does SyncSocio show?**
A: Live data pulled from Instagram Graph API: Total Reach, Weekly Reach, Impressions, Engagement Rate, Likes This Week, Total Followers, Follower Growth (30-day), Posts This Month, and period-on-period percentage changes.

**Q: How is Engagement Rate calculated?**
A: Engagement Rate = ((Likes + Comments + Saves) divided by Total Reach) x 100. It weights all interactions, not just likes, for a meaningful signal.

**Q: Why does the Analytics page show an error or no data?**
A: Analytics requires your Instagram Business account to be connected. Go to Settings > Connected Accounts and ensure Instagram shows as "Connected." If connected and still failing, your Instagram access token may have expired — reconnect it.

**Q: What does a positive percentage mean (e.g. +12.4%)?**
A: It means this period's metric is higher than the same metric in the previous period. A rising number means your content is performing well.

**Q: Why is my analytics data from a few hours ago?**
A: Instagram's API has caching built in. Data is refreshed every 60 seconds in SyncSocio, but Instagram itself may take up to 2-3 hours to surface the latest metrics for new posts.

## 6. DM AUTOMATION

**Q: What is DM Automation?**
A: DM Automation lets you build keyword-triggered reply flows on Instagram. When someone comments a specific word (e.g. "price") on your post, or replies to your story, the bot instantly sends them a custom DM — with a product link, offer, or follow-up sequence.

**Q: Which plans include DM Automation?**
A: Growth and Pro plans. Not available on Starter.

**Q: How do I create a DM Automation flow?**
A: Go to DM Automation in the sidebar. Click "New Flow," set your trigger type (Comment Keyword, Story Reply, or DM Keyword), enter your keyword, write your DM sequence, and activate the flow. The bot will start working immediately.

**Q: What trigger types are available?**
A: Comment Keyword, Story Reply, and DM Keyword. You can also target "All Posts" or a specific post.

**Q: What can I include in a DM flow?**
A: Your DM flow can include text messages, buttons (URL links, follow prompt, next message in sequence), and conditional branching (e.g., show a different message if the user is not following you yet).

**Q: My DM flow is not sending. Why?**
A: Common causes: (1) Flow is not activated — check the toggle is ON, (2) Instagram token expired — reconnect in Settings, (3) your comment keyword was not matched — it is case-insensitive but must be an exact word match in the comment, (4) Meta may be restricting automated messages on your account. If the issue persists, raise a ticket.

**Q: Can I use DM Automation for WhatsApp?**
A: Yes. Growth and Pro plans include WhatsApp DM automation alongside Instagram.

## 7. NEXA AI SALES AGENT

**Q: What is Nexa AI Sales Agent?**
A: Nexa is your AI-powered Instagram DM sales agent. It reads incoming DM conversations from leads and potential customers, generates context-aware replies in your brand's tone, and can take payments, send product links, and close sales — all automatically in Instagram DMs.

**Q: Which plans include Nexa AI Sales Agent?**
A: Growth (Razorpay + UPI payments, capped conversations) and Pro (Unlimited conversations, Razorpay + UPI + COD payments). Nexa is NOT available on Starter.

**Q: How do I set up Nexa?**
A: Go to Settings > Nexa AI Setup. Fill in your Business Name, Products/Services descriptions, Pricing, FAQs, and preferred tone. Once saved, Nexa will use this information when replying to DMs. You also need Instagram to be connected.

**Q: What information should I add in Nexa AI Setup?**
A: Add: your business name, description of your products/services, your prices (with any payment links or catalog links), your WhatsApp number if you want leads redirected there, common customer FAQs (e.g., "Do you ship to Delhi?"), and your preferred reply tone.

**Q: Can Nexa take payments?**
A: Yes. Growth plan includes Razorpay + UPI payment collection via DM. Pro plan adds COD (Cash on Delivery) payment flow. Configure your Razorpay keys in Settings > Nexa AI Setup.

**Q: How does Nexa learn from conversations?**
A: When you manually reply to a conversation from the Nexa Agent page with "Learn from this reply" enabled, Nexa extracts a generalized Q&A pair and adds it to your brand's knowledge base. Future conversations benefit from this learning automatically.

**Q: My Nexa is not replying to DMs. Why?**
A: Check: (1) Is Instagram connected? (2) Does your plan include Nexa? (3) Is the Nexa profile in Settings > Nexa AI Setup saved and complete? (4) Is the DM from a real Instagram user (not a test account)? If all good and still not working, raise a support ticket.

**Q: Can I see and manage Nexa's conversations?**
A: Yes. Go to Nexa Agent in the sidebar. You can see all conversations, read the full DM thread, send manual replies, and toggle "Learn from this reply" to teach Nexa your preferences.

**Q: What happens if Nexa doesn't know the answer?**
A: Nexa will try to find the best response from your Knowledge Base. If it genuinely cannot help, it will tell the customer a human will follow up — and flag the conversation for your review.

## 8. WHATSAPP AND EMAIL CAMPAIGNS

**Q: How do I run a WhatsApp Campaign?**
A: Go to Studio > WhatsApp Campaign mode. Upload your contact list (Excel/CSV), pick a WhatsApp-approved template (or generate one with AI), and send. WhatsApp campaigns use Meta's WhatsApp Business API — costs are Meta's messaging charges, not SyncSocio credits.

**Q: How do I run an Email Campaign?**
A: Go to Studio > Email Campaign mode. Write your email copy (or let AI generate it), upload your contact list, and send. Starter plan does not include email campaigns. Growth: 500 emails/month. Pro: 2,000 emails/month.

**Q: How do I set up a business email for campaigns?**
A: You need to configure your SMTP settings in Settings > Email Setup, or verify your domain. Watch the step-by-step guide: https://youtu.be/6C93iRe5l34

**Q: My email campaign is not sending. Why?**
A: Check your SMTP settings in Settings > Email Setup. Ensure you've entered the correct SMTP host, port, username, and app password. Gmail users must use an App Password, not their main password.

## 9. PRODUCTS AND ASSET LIBRARY

**Q: What is the Product Library?**
A: The Product Library (under Studio > Products) is a catalog where you store your products with images, names, prices, and descriptions. During any generation, you can pick a product from this catalog — no need to re-enter details every time.

**Q: What is the Asset Library?**
A: The Asset Library stores all your generated and saved content — images, carousels, videos. Everything is organized by type and date. Assets never expire. You can re-download, reuse, or schedule any saved asset at any time.

**Q: How many products can I add to the catalog?**
A: Starter: 50 products. Growth: 200 products. Pro: 500 products.

## 10. CREDITS

**Q: How do credits work?**
A: Credits are consumed each time you generate AI content. Typical costs: 5 credits per SD image (Starter/Growth), 8 credits per HD image (Pro). Carousel and video generations cost proportionally more. Your plan includes a monthly credit allowance.

**Q: How many credits do I get per plan?**
A: Starter: 80 credits/month (approx 16 SD images). Growth: 400 credits/month (approx 80 SD images). Pro: 1,000 credits/month (approx 125 HD images).

**Q: Do unused credits carry over?**
A: No. Credits reset at the start of each billing cycle and don't carry over. Use them throughout the month.

**Q: How do I buy more credits (top-up)?**
A: Go to Settings > Plan and Billing > Buy Credits, or click the credit counter at the top of the dashboard. Top-up packs available: 100 credits (Rs 299 / $5), 300 credits (Rs 799 / $12), 700 credits (Rs 1,699 / $24).

**Q: My credits deducted but no image was generated.**
A: This is rare and usually a network error. Please raise a support ticket with the approximate time of the failed generation so we can verify and refund the credits if appropriate.

## 12. INSTAGRAM CONNECTION

**Q: How do I connect my Instagram account?**
A: Go to Settings > Connected Accounts. Click "Connect Instagram." You'll be redirected to Facebook to authorize the connection. Your Instagram account must be a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page.

**Q: Why is Instagram showing as disconnected?**
A: Instagram access tokens expire periodically. Go to Settings > Connected Accounts and click "Reconnect Instagram" to refresh the token.

**Q: How do I set up an Instagram Business or Creator account?**
A: Follow our setup guide: https://www.syncsocio.com/instagram-setup

**Q: My Instagram is connected but analytics shows no data.**
A: The Instagram Graph API requires your account to have at least 100 followers and some post activity to surface analytics data. If your account is very new, wait a few days. If your account is established, reconnect Instagram in Settings.

**Q: What is Meta Tester status?**
A: If your app is in Meta review (your Instagram is not fully approved for all API permissions yet), you will see a "Meta Tester" badge. Some functionality may be limited. Contact support if your Meta review has been pending for over 5 business days.

## 14. BILLING AND PAYMENTS

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: All major UPI apps (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm), Debit/Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), Net Banking, and Wallets — via Razorpay. International payments via Stripe.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
A: Yes. All payments are processed by Razorpay (India) or Stripe (international) — PCI-DSS compliant gateways. SyncSocio never stores your card details.

**Q: I was charged but my plan didn't activate. What do I do?**
A: This can happen if the payment succeeded but the callback failed. Raise a support ticket with your Razorpay Payment ID (found in your bank or UPI statement) and we will activate your plan manually within a few hours.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: SyncSocio has a strict no-refund policy for digital products (plans and credits). However, if you were charged in error or experienced a technical failure, please raise a ticket and we review it case-by-case within 3 business days.
